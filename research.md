## Relevant Industry Standards or Protocols

- **Deal Registration** — standardized process allowing partners to register deals with vendors to claim margin protection; nearly universal requirement in channel programs; PRM tools must enforce registration workflows with duplicate-detection logic
- **Market Development Funds (MDF)** — vendor-funded co-marketing budget programs managed through PRMs; co-op vs. MDF accounting conventions vary by vendor; tools must support claim submission, approval, and proof-of-performance workflows
- **Salesforce Experience Cloud (Community Cloud) standard** — many enterprise PRMs either extend Salesforce or must integrate with it as the partner-facing portal
- **OAuth 2.0 / SAML 2.0** — SSO standards required for partner portal authentication across enterprise partner bases
- **GDPR / CCPA / data residency requirements** — partner contact data and deal data span multiple jurisdictions; compliance is complex in global channel programs
- **Co-sell programs (AWS, Microsoft, Google)** — cloud hyperscaler co-sell certification and deal registration APIs (ACE, Microsoft Partner Center, Google Cloud Partner Advantage) are increasingly required integrations for tech-company PRMs

## AI-Native Opportunity

- **Partner onboarding is slow and repetitive:** enterprise vendors report 3–6 month partner onboarding cycles; AI could auto-generate customized training paths, portal walkthroughs, and certification sequences based on partner business profile, existing product knowledge, and region — reducing time-to-first-deal dramatically
- **Deal registration is a black box for partners:** partners submit deals but get minimal feedback on probability, competitive context, or recommended co-sell resources; an AI-native PRM could surface deal coaching (likely to win, missing information, suggested contacts) at registration time
- **Co-sell orchestration across cloud marketplaces is highly manual:** AWS ACE, Microsoft Partner Center, and Google Cloud Partner Advantage each have separate portals and APIs; an AI-native tool that unified co-sell tracking across all three hyperscalers and auto-synced deal status would address a genuine underserved pain point
- **No open-source PRM exists with meaningful adoption:** xAmplify claims open-source but lacks community; an OSS PRM with solid deal registration, portal, and CRM sync would capture the startup-to-mid-market segment priced out of Impartner/Allbound and unwilling to build on Salesforce Experience Cloud
- **Partner performance analytics are lagging indicators:** current PRMs show deal volume and revenue-per-partner historically; AI could predict which partners are likely to churn from the program, which are underperforming due to enablement gaps vs. market fit issues, and which new prospects would make high-performing partners
